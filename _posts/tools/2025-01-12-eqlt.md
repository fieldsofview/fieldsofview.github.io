objective: To enable organisation measure effectiveness of social protection schemes at the household level and understand gaps in household resilience.
description: E-QLT is a simulation tool that helps quantify vulnerabilities faced by the household, test shocks and measure contribution of social protection measures towards household resilience.
summary: <p>
  Organisation can use the open platform where they can use the data of the communities they are working with, and use the scenarios they are interested in. They can look at the resilience of the communities at multiple levels (household, group and community) and download the reports.
  </p>
  <p>
  If the organisations want further customisation and scenarios, we can work together to build custom version of the simulation.
  </p>
usecase: <ul>
    <li> To understand how the communities are vulnerable and contribution existing social protection measures. </li>
    <li> Using the report generated towards evidence based advocacy for strenghtening resilience. </li>
    <li> To plan for resilience among the vulnerable communities. </li>
  </ul>
