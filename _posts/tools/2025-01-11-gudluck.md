objective: To understand the impact of climate change and adaptation strategies on a household's well-being 
description: X is a capacity-building game where the participants navigate through different seasons, investing in climate adaptation strategies to address their household impacts before they become unaffordable, or too difficult to fully recover from.
summary: <p>
  Organisations can use the game to build community capacity about the impacts of climate change and the effect of various adaptation strategies on household health and finance. The game is to be implemented within the organisation's training programs, facilitated by trainers.
  </p>
  <p>
  The game can be customised with relevant data relevant for the target audience and can accommodate various types of strategies, including schemes and policies.
  </p>
usecase: <ul>
    <li> To understand the impact of climate change and adaptation strategies on a hosuehold's health and finances. </li>
    <li> To assess community preferences and behaviour patterns regarding investment in climate adaptation strategies. </li>
    <li> For communities to test  various adaptation strategies, helping them make informed decisions for their households. </li>
  </ul>
impact: The game enhances awareness and builds individuals' capacity around climate change and available adaptation strategies, empowering them to make informed decisions to improve their household resilience.  It strengthens communities' ability to assess and choose effective strategies, ultimately increasing climate resilience at the household level.
