<br>
For the workshop, we used a modified version of our flagship tool, <a href="https://eqlt.in/" target="_blank" style="mso-line-height-rule: exactly;-ms-text-size-adjust: 100%;-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%;color: #007C89;font-weight: normal;text-decoration: underline;">E-QLT</a>, a <strong>data-driven simulation tool</strong> that uses a system dynamics modelling approach to model a household based on its member composition, income, expenditure, exposure to shocks, and access to social protection schemes. &nbsp;<br>
<br>
Using the tool, participants <strong>quantitatively assessed the vulnerability</strong> of their chosen household over a 5-year period in terms of a <strong>Social Protection Score (SPS)</strong>, a credit score-like metric that we developed to express household resilience across the dimensions of <strong>finance, health, and education</strong>.
