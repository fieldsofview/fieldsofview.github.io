We want to pay it forward

Fields of View has sustained itself thanks to a rich community of mentors, advisors, and partners who have been generous with their time and patient with our questions. Over the years, they have offered us many new avenues to learn and helped make our idea of FoV a reality. Our journey has been that much easier and more enjoyable because of the support we received when we started out.

Now, we want to pay it forward.

We invite aspiring social entrepreneurs, innovators, and change-makers to join us at our collaborative space in JP Nagar, Bangalore. Whether you're just starting your organisation or looking at growth, whether you're a for-profit or nonprofit initiative, at Fields of View, you will always find a diverse group of professionals eager to engage with your ideas amid passionate discussion and lively banter.

Our home in JP Nagar is also available for you to hold meetings, screen movies, convene a book club, or host small workshops. We have a high-ceiling room on the ground floor and a sunny terrace complete with an enclosed glass-walled room. Our library boasts an enviable collection of books and board games that are sure to keep you entertained and enthused.

In the spirit of paying it forward, you do not have to pay us a fee; all we ask is that you respect the house rules.

Accessibility Details
We are based in an old house with multiple levels. The entrance to the ground floor has one step. This level houses our kitchen, primary meeting spaces, and largest discussion area, all barrier-free once inside. The ground floor also contains a small toilet; however, it is not wheelchair accessible.

Upper floors are accessible via staircase. They including a mezzanine room, two first-floor rooms, and a glass-windowed terrace space. The first floor also contains a bathroom.
